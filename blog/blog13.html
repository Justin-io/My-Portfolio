Neuromorphic Computing: Brain Chips

Nov 25, 2024 Hari Nandan K Lab Ref: 2026-B13
Architectural Takeaway

Von Neumann architectures suffer from memory-bus bottlenecks. Neuromorphic silicon collocates compute and memory in artificial synapses, firing asynchronous spikes at milliwatt power.

1. Overcoming the Von Neumann Memory Bottleneck

Our brains consume 20 watts. Supercomputers consume megawatts. Neuromorphic chips like Intel Loihi try to bridge this gap using spiking neural networks.
