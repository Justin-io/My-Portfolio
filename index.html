AI SYSTEMS / RAG BENCHMARKING

Enterprise RAG Scientific Benchmark Platform

Comprehensive evaluation and benchmarking suite designed to stress-test retrieval pipelines against complex enterprise document corpora before production deployment.

Problem
Enterprise engineering teams deploy RAG pipelines blindly without empirical benchmarking, suffering high hallucination rates, fragile retrieval across dense PDFs, and silent production regressions.
Role
AI Systems Architect & Benchmarking Lead (Pipeline Topologies, Metric Harness, Eval Scripts, Statistical Validation).
Architecture
Scientific evaluation framework benchmarking 6 canonical topologies (Naive, Hybrid BM25+Dense, ColBERT Reranked, HyDE, GraphRAG, and Agentic RAG) with context grounding, adversarial noise injection, and statistical significance scoring.
Evaluation Method
Automated RAGAS and TruLens metrics (faithfulness, answer relevance, context recall) paired with bootstrap confidence intervals on adversarial noise corpora.
Evidenced Results
Quantified retrieval trade-offs across 6 topologies; boosted context relevance by 42% on noisy enterprise corpora compared to standard dense retrieval.
Limitations
GraphRAG index construction exhibits higher upfront latency during initial document ingestion due to entity resolution overhead (O(V+E)).

Autonomous industrial equipment health auditing and anomaly detection platform engineered for chiller plants, energy infrastructure, and continuous IoT monitoring.

Architecture
8-layer physical and mathematical auditing pipeline combining expected-behavior multivariate regression, ASHRAE Guideline 14 measurement-and-verification (M&V) thermal baseline modeling, zero-trust Evidence Critic guardrails, and streaming reasoning.
Evaluation Method
